Our story

A ready-mix supplier

that grew plant by plant.

3Q began as a small independent ready-mix supplier in Ledig. Through steady growth it expanded from a single plant into a network that supplies Johannesburg, Pretoria, Rustenburg, Brits, eMalahleni and Middelburg.

We are a BBBEE compliant company focused on commercial and contract ready-mix. Our experience and flexibility let us give you the right guidance and consistent quality concrete on schedule, so you can manage deadlines and budgets with confidence.
